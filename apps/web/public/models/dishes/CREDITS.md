# 3D model credits

All models are built from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) assets released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**; no attribution is required, but credit is given here anyway. Models were re-centred and grounded; the tea set was rearranged and the composites were assembled from the assets listed. Thumbnails in `public/images/dishes/` are the Poly Haven asset thumbnails, except `fruit-bowl.png` and `lemonade.png`, which were rendered from the GLBs with model-viewer.

- `croissant.glb`: [Croissant](https://polyhaven.com/a/croissant) (Dario Barresi, Greg Zaal), CC0
- `carrot-cake.glb`: [Carrot Cake](https://polyhaven.com/a/carrot_cake) (Greg Zaal, James Ray Cock), CC0
- `strawberry-chocolate-cake.glb`: [Strawberry Chocolate Cake](https://polyhaven.com/a/strawberry_chocolate_cake) (Kuutti Siitonen), CC0
- `afternoon-tea.glb`: [Tea Set 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/tea_set_01) (Rico Cilliers, James Ray Cock, Jurita Burger), CC0
- `fruit-bowl.glb`: [Wooden Bowl 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_bowl_01), [Food Apple 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/food_apple_01), [Food Kiwi 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/food_kiwi_01), [Food Pomegranate 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/food_pomegranate_01), [Food Lychee 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/food_lychee_01) (Oliver Harries) and [Pears Asian 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/food_pears_asian_01) (Amal Kumar), all CC0
