Research date: 27 Oct 2026. Older data is marked with its year. "Estimate" or "assumption" in the Inferences sections marks numbers I did not find in a source; the inputs are shown. Many sources here are vendor blogs (Petpooja, Restroworks, DineOpen) or data aggregators (Inc42 company pages, Payscale, AmbitionBox); they are flagged where it matters.

## 1. How did Indian restaurant-tech companies acquire their first 100–1,000 restaurants?

### Takeaway
No founder account I found describes the literal "first 100 restaurants" for Petpooja, DotPe, Restroworks, UrbanPiper, Thrive or Rista. What the evidence does show is three different routes. (a) Petpooja: cheap per-outlet pricing, field sales, word of mouth, a 15% reseller programme, and a focus on small and Tier 3/4 restaurants. (b) Restroworks/Posist and DotPe: anchor logos first, meaning enterprise chains, malls and food courts. (c) UrbanPiper and Thrive: riding aggregator pain or aggregator ties, plus backing from strategic investors with distribution (Swiggy/Zomato, Coca-Cola, Jubilant). Payment companies show up as integration partners. I found no evidence that any of these companies used them as the primary acquisition channel.

### Cited Findings
**Petpooja (Ahmedabad; SMB, high volume)**
- Began in 2011 in Ahmedabad as a B2B or corporate meal-delivery middleman. Founders Parthiv and Apurv Patel later pivoted to restaurant software — [The Captable, Feb 2025](https://the-captable.com/2025/02/petpooja-saas-restaurant-software-foodtech-cloud-kitchen/). A 2021 YourStory article says it started offering a "simple billing point-of-sale solution" in 2014 — [YourStory, Nov 2021](https://yourstory.com/2021/11/point-of-sale-fnb-petpooja-funding-udaan)
- Target segment is small restaurants (family-run eateries and small chains with roughly Rs 50 lakh to Rs 5 crore annual revenue). The company grew on pricing and word of mouth (no figures given; the article is paywalled) — [The Captable, Feb 2025](https://the-captable.com/2025/02/petpooja-saas-restaurant-software-foodtech-cloud-kitchen/)
- (2022) "The platform has scaled from 1200 customers a month in the pre-pandemic days to more than 3000 customers a month today", with about 35k customers in India and "more than 50% of outlets from Tier 3 and 4 regions". Planned partnerships included Paytm payment devices and ONDC — [Passionate in Marketing, 16 Aug 2022](https://www.passionateinmarketing.com/?p=467385)
- Reseller programme: resellers "earn 15% of assured revenue" as a straight commission on each conversion, credited 10–12 days after the client onboards. Existing Petpooja POS users get a ₹500 referral bonus. The page does not say whether the commission is first-year only or recurring — [Petpooja Reseller Program](https://www.petpooja.com/reseller-program)
- Field sales is still central. A Petpooja Field Sales Executive posting (Surat, July 2025) describes "lead generation through field visits and cold calling, conducting client demos, onboarding restaurants, and maintaining long-term client relationships", at ₹4.2–5 lakh/yr — [Expertia job listing](https://www.expertia.ai/petpooja/job/hiring-field-sales-executive-6874cd64e348db1b7ef1366c)
- Pricing: Petpooja's official pricing page lists POS Core at US$800 for the first year per outlet, Plus at $1,600 and Scale at $2,000, and notes that "prices might vary based on location" (this looks like a non-India price list) — [Petpooja pricing](https://petpooja.com/pricing). Indian listings give Rs 10,000 for the first year per outlet and Rs 7,500/yr on renewal — [Techjockey](https://techjockey.com/detail/petpooja-pos); the same figures appear in an older listing (last updated March 2020) — [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/petpooja/pricing). A competitor's 2026 estimate (from user reports, not official) says ₹3,000–12,000/month before GST — [DineOpen](https://www.dineopen.com/blog/petpooja-pricing-plans-2026.html)
- Scale: 1,00,000+ clients across India, the UAE and South Africa; processes ~25% of online order volume on Zomato and Swiggy; Rs 137 crore ($15.5M) Series C led by Dharana Capital at roughly Rs 910 crore valuation — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/petpooja-raises-155-mn-series-c-at-over-3x-valuation-premium-10512396)
- FY24 revenue about ₹77.2 Cr (FY23 ₹54.2 Cr, +42.4%), FY24 net loss ₹13.4 Cr (aggregator data) — [Inc42 financials](https://inc42.com/company/petpooja/financials/)
- Payment-company ties take the form of integrations. Petpooja "Pay+" runs card machines from Paytm, Razorpay, Pine Labs, Mosambee and BharatPe, with auto-settlement and reconciliation — [Petpooja blog](https://blog.petpooja.com/industry-business-guides/petpooja-finance-money-layer-in-pos/)

**DotPe (Gurgaon; QR "Scan-Order-Pay", fintech-backed)**
- In mid-2020 (the COVID contactless period), DotPe announced anchor deals: McDonald's India North & East in Delhi NCR (Aug 2020) — [Restaurant India](https://www.restaurantindia.in/news/mcdonalds-north-east-partners-with-dotpe-for-contactless-ordering.n19410); 12 F&B brands in the Phoenix Marketcity Bengaluru food court — [Restaurant India](https://www.restaurantindia.in/news/phoenix-marketcity-bengaluru-adopts-contactless-dining-and-take-away-team-up-with-dotpe.n19401); and Nexus malls. The solution was reportedly used by Starbucks, McDonald's, Haldiram's, Social, Chili's and Barista, with a stated goal of 1 million merchants — [Retail4Growth, Jul 2020](https://www.retail4growth.com/news/lite/contactless-future-dotpe-to-enable-contactless-dining-and-take-aways-at-nexus-malls-5148)
- (2022) Backed by PayU, Info Edge Ventures and Google; in talks for ~$50M led by Temasek. Restaurants "scan their inventories" into WhatsApp-accessible menus for direct ordering. Merchant counts and revenue model were not disclosed — [TechCrunch, May 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/31/temasek-in-talks-to-invest-in-google-backed-dotpe/)
- I found no source describing how DotPe priced for or acquired SMB merchants. A Dealroom profile only speculates that it uses "a subscription or transaction fee model" — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/dot_2)

**Restroworks (formerly Posist; enterprise-first)**
- Onboarded enterprise groups Lite Bite Foods, K Hospitality and First Fiddle when it launched its full-stack enterprise platform, then Taco Bell, Sbarro, Belgian Waffle Co and other chains — [Restroworks About](https://www.restroworks.com/about-us/)
- Founder Ashish Tulsian came to restaurant tech through a side investment in a restaurant; his interview focuses on user research — [Slingshot Interview #6](https://slingshot.substack.com/p/slingshot-interview-6-startups-angel)
- At the 2024 rebrand: "over 80 percent year-over-year growth", 20,000 customers in 50+ countries — [Restroworks news, 2024](https://www.restroworks.com/news/posist-restroworks-rebranding-2024/). It now states 25,000+ restaurants, founded 2012 — [Restroworks LLM info](https://www.restroworks.com/llm-info/)

**UrbanPiper (Bengaluru; aggregator-integration middleware)**
- Started as a two-person team in a Bengaluru co-working space and built the product with feedback from restaurateurs. Bootstrapped for about 1.5 years and revenue-positive before a first investment from Axilor Ventures. Founding year is 2015 or 2016 depending on the source — [UrbanPiper About](https://www.urbanpiper.com/about/); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/?p=61557)
- $24M Series B led by Sequoia India and Tiger Global, with Swiggy and Zomato participating. Reports cited 27,000+ locations in 8 countries — [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/en-in/finance/urbanpiper-raises-24-million-in-series-b-funding-led-by/425012)

**Thrive (Mumbai-first; direct ordering against aggregators)**
- Charges a 3% commission (vs ~20% for Swiggy/Zomato) for restaurant micro-sites. Restaurants get their customer data, and delivery runs on third-party logistics. Claimed 14,000 restaurants in about two years, "primarily in Mumbai". Coca-Cola India took a 15% stake — [Finshots, 21 Apr 2023](https://finshots.in/archive/is-thrive-going-to-thrive-in-the-foodtech-space/); [BeverageDaily, Apr 2023](https://www.beveragedaily.com/Article/2023/04/18/Coca-Cola-India-invests-in-food-delivery-company-Thrive/)
- Its Razorpay app-store listing says dine-in is commission-free and that 9,500+ restaurants in 76 cities use it (this conflicts with the 14,000 figure; dates unclear) — [Razorpay App Store](https://razorpay.com/app-store/thrive)

**Rista (Bengaluru; small, bootstrapped)**
- Founded 2016 in Bengaluru by Srini Vasan, bootstrapped, about 15 employees, focused on multi-brand cloud-kitchen management (single aggregator source) — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/company/rista-mobility/). Capterra lists DotPe as the vendor for Rista, which suggests a distribution or ownership tie-up I could not verify — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1073288/Rista)

**HungerBox (Bengaluru; corporate cafeterias)**
- (2020) Bengaluru-based, started 2016, Paytm-backed. It digitised corporate cafeterias (Microsoft, Accenture, Amazon, Qualcomm and others) and claimed 120+ corporate clients in 14 cities and 560,000 orders/day — [YourStory, Mar 2020](https://yourstory.com/2020/03/paytm-growthstory-kris-gopalakrishnan-backed-bengaluru-b2b-food-startup-hungerbox). An undated profile says 530 cafeteria food courts, 176 clients and 180M meals — [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/companies/hungerbox/)

**AR-menu precedent**
- (2017) Kabaq, an early AR food startup, charged restaurants US$150–199/month, shot food in restaurants and processed it with photogrammetry on its own servers — [YourStory, Jul 2017](https://yourstory.com/2017/07/cant-decide-order-kabaq-makes-easy-putting-virtual-3d-food-table/)

### Inferences
- Petpooja's revenue per client implies a low ARPU. Estimate: FY24 revenue ₹77.2 Cr ÷ roughly 1,00,000 clients (2025 count, so the years don't match) ≈ ₹7,700 per client per year. That is consistent with the ₹7,500 renewal price. Restaurant owners are anchored on a POS costing well under ₹1,000/month. DineView's SaaS fee will be compared against that anchor unless it is framed as a revenue (upsell) tool rather than an operations tool.
- The 15% reseller commission is an Indian channel benchmark. On a ₹30,000/yr DineView contract (illustrative only), a 15% channel payout is ₹4,500 per outlet.
- DotPe and Restroworks show the value of a few recognisable Bengaluru logos (a mall food court or a mid-size chain) as proof points. DotPe's 2020 Phoenix Marketcity Bengaluru food-court deal (12 brands in one contract) is the closest precedent for a "food court as a cluster" play.
- Payment companies (Paytm, Pine Labs, PhonePe, BharatPe) appear in the evidence as integration partners and planned distribution (Petpooja's 2022 plan for "Paytm payment devices"). I found no evidence that a restaurant-SaaS startup got its first customers through a soundbox or POS bundle. Treat that route as unproven for a seed-stage company.

### Gaps
- No primary account (podcast or interview transcript) of how Petpooja, DotPe, UrbanPiper or Thrive closed their first 100–1,000 restaurants: sales team size, founder-led selling, or the share of free vs paid accounts.
- No DotPe pricing or free-tier history was found.
- No data on payment-company co-selling deals (Paytm, PhonePe or Pine Labs reselling restaurant SaaS).

## 2. Benchmarks: sales cycle, CAC, churn, restaurant closure rate, field-sales rep cost, demo-to-close (India vs global)

### Takeaway
India-specific SaaS benchmarks for restaurants are thin. The most reliable Indian data points are field-sales pay (roughly ₹3–6 lakh/yr base for restaurant-tech roles) and a 15% reseller commission. India has no official restaurant failure rate. Global SMB SaaS benchmarks are 3–7% monthly churn, a 15–40 day sales cycle and an 18–32% demo-to-close rate. Plan with those as planning ranges, not as Indian facts.

### Cited Findings
**Restaurant closure rate**
- "No government body, industry association, or ratings agency in India publishes a restaurant failure rate". Licences record openings, not closures. The same blog summarises US data: 26% of restaurants failed in year one, 19% in year two and 14% in year three, 59% cumulative within three years (Columbus, Ohio, 2,439 restaurants, 1996–1999, H.G. Parsa). A US BLS census found 17% first-year failure for independent full-service restaurants. It calls the popular "90% fail in year one" and "60% in year one" claims unsupported — [Petpooja blog (2022, updated Sep 2026)](https://blog.petpooja.com/?p=23372)
- (FY21, pandemic) NRAI reported that the sector shrank 53% and over 25% of outlets shut permanently (via Business Standard, as cited in) — [Petpooja blog](https://blog.petpooja.com/?p=23372)
- (Aug 2020, pandemic) A Zomato survey found 10% of restaurants permanently shut and projected another 30% might not reopen — [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/amp/bt-newsflicks/photo/covid-19-impact-83-restaurants-not-open-10-permanently-shut-claims-survey-266199-2020-08-26); [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/atom/40-restaurants-india-may-never-reopen-again-zomato-report-131163)
- A Restroworks blog FAQ states "about 60% of new restaurants shut down within their first year" with no source or year. Treat it as unsupported (the Petpooja blog explicitly calls this figure unsourced) — [Restroworks blog (Jun 2025)](https://www.restroworks.com/blog/indian-restaurant-industry-statistics/)

**India SMB SaaS CAC, churn and payback (weak sources)**
- Indian SMB SaaS: CAC ~$80–250, annual churn 15–25%, payback 18–36 months, ACV $200–800/yr. No methodology is given — [eFinancialModels](https://www.efinancialmodels.com/?p=653269)
- Indian B2B SaaS (₹10–30 Cr ARR band): blended CAC ₹1.2L–2.5L, LTV:CAC 2.5–3x, payback 14–20 months. Indian ACVs run 40–60% below the US while sales cycles are similar. Based on the consultancy's own clients and not SMB-specific — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/?p=58566)
- I found no SaaSBOOMi or Bessemer India SMB churn/CAC benchmark. McKinsey's India SaaS report (with SaaSBOOMi involvement) did not show these numbers in what I could access — [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/technology-media-and-telecommunications/our-insights/turning-india-into-a-saas-power)

**Field-sales rep cost in Bengaluru (India data)**
- Payscale (2026, all field sales roles in Bangalore): average base ~₹3,00,000/yr, range ~₹1.67L–₹7.83L, commission ₹0–20k/yr — [Payscale](https://www.payscale.com/research/IN/Job=Field_Sales_Executive/Salary/409168cf/Bangalore). Early-career: ~₹2,96,130 base, commission up to ~₹70k — [Payscale early-career](https://www.payscale.com/research/IN/Job=Field_Sales_Executive/Salary/5f6877fe/Early-Career-Bangalore)
- Bengaluru job postings: an HSR Layout field sales role selling to corporate/commercial F&B pays ₹15,000–25,000/month fixed plus travel allowance plus incentives — [JobHai/Corpiz](https://www.jobhai.com/hi/field-sales-field-executive-job-in-corpiz-services-private-limited-hsr-layout-bangalore-0-to-1-years-1784899606-8041264-jid). A frozen-food role calling on hotel and restaurant purchase managers pays ₹28,000–29,000/month plus commission — [TeamLease/Niranjan](https://teamlease.com/jobs/field-sales-executive-jobs-in-bangalore-niranjan-1725468)
- Petpooja comparables (not Bengaluru-specific): Relationship Manager ₹2.8–6.5 L/yr (self-reported, 265 employees) — [AmbitionBox](https://ambitionbox.com/jobs/petpooja-jobs); Business Development Executive ₹2.6–6.5 LPA (avg ₹3.8 LPA), RM avg ₹5.3 LPA — [Talentd](https://www.talentd.in/companies/petpooja?salary_band=all); Field Sales Executive (Surat, Jul 2025) ₹4.2–5 L/yr — [Expertia](https://www.expertia.ai/petpooja/job/hiring-field-sales-executive-6874cd64e348db1b7ef1366c)

**Global SMB SaaS benchmarks (not India-specific)**
- SMB monthly churn 3–7% (≈36–76% annually) — [Kalungi](https://kalungi.com/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks). "Good" B2B SMB monthly churn is 2.5–5% (ProfitWell/Lenny Rachitsky, as summarised) — [Enrich Labs](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/churn-rate-complete-guide-2026). Very small businesses churn "3–5% a month often" — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/which-is-the-churn-benchmark-for-saas). SMB (10–99 employees) churn is 6.4% monthly and concentrates in the first 90 days — [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-churn-rate-by-industry-saas/)
- Demo-to-close: SMB (1–50 employees) average 32%, top performers 40%+, SMB cycle 15–30 days (data from 939 B2B companies; vendor blog) — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/saas-demo-best-practices-2026.md). Median demo-to-close 18.7% across 18,400 demos, all segments blended — [Visionary Marketing](https://visionary-marketing.co.uk/blog/b2b-demo-conversion-statistics-2026). SMB SaaS deals under $5k close in 30–90 days, median 40 days — [Digital Bloom](https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/pipeline-performance-benchmarks-2025/)

### Inferences
- **Churn floor from closures (estimate):** converting the US first-year failure rates to monthly gives 1−(1−0.26)^(1/12) ≈ 2.5%/month (Parsa, 1996–99) and 1−(1−0.17)^(1/12) ≈ 1.5%/month (BLS independents). This is involuntary churn among *new* restaurants alone, before any churn from dissatisfaction. Indian rates are unknown; there is no reason to assume they are lower. Plan base-case total logo churn at 3–5%/month (global SMB band), and model a 6–7% stress case.
- **Rep cost (estimate, inputs shown):** fixed pay of ₹25,000–42,000/month (Payscale average ₹3L/yr = ₹25k/mo; Petpooja field sales ₹4.2–5L/yr = ₹35–42k/mo), plus incentives and travel. Assumption, unsourced: ₹5,000–10,000/month for petrol, phone and incentives. That gives a loaded cost of roughly ₹30,000–52,000 per rep per month.
- **CAC per outlet (illustrative):** CAC = loaded rep cost ÷ closes per month. Assumptions: about 25 qualified demos a month per rep, and close rates of 18.7–32% (global SMB, not India-validated), giving ~5–8 closes a month. CAC ≈ ₹30k–52k ÷ 5–8 ≈ **₹3,750–10,400 per outlet** in rep cost alone. Add pilot hardware (QR standees ₹80–150 per table — see §5) and 3D capture if it is subsidised. The 25-demos figure and the transfer of global close rates to India are assumptions to validate in month 1–2.
- **Payback (illustrative):** at a hypothetical ₹2,500/outlet/month price (between Petpooja's ~₹625–833/month and the ₹3,300–5,000/month AR-platform range in §5), a ₹7,500 CAC pays back in about 3 months of gross revenue. At 4% monthly churn, expected lifetime is ~25 months, so revenue LTV ≈ ₹62,500 and LTV:CAC ≈ 8x before COGS and support. This is far better than the upGrowth Indian B2B average (2.5–3x), which suggests the model's weak point is price acceptance and churn, not CAC.

### Gaps
- No India-specific restaurant SaaS sales cycle, demo-to-close rate or monthly logo churn from a primary source (SaaSBOOMi, Bessemer or Chiratae reports were not found or not accessible).
- No official Indian restaurant closure rate exists, per the Petpooja blog. I found no academic Indian cohort study either.
- No Bengaluru-specific pay data for restaurant-SaaS field reps; the Petpooja figures are from other cities.

## 3. Bengaluru-specific channels (associations, events, influencers, tech parks, hotels)

### Takeaway
The strongest Bengaluru-specific channel in 2026 is the Bruhat Bengaluru Hotels Association (BBHA). In July–August 2026 it organised 1,000+ owners against Swiggy/Zomato commissions of 8–28%. That gives DineView a timely "own your dine-in guest, raise the bill" message. Other channels: the WOHXpo (formerly Food Hospitality World) show at Palace Grounds in June, the FHRAI convention in Bengaluru (Sep 2026), micro food creators, and mall and corporate food courts (DotPe's 2020 Phoenix Marketcity deal; HungerBox in corporate cafeterias).

### Cited Findings
**BBHA**
- On 29 Jul 2026 the BBHA announced it would boycott Swiggy and Zomato from 15 Aug. BBHA president **S Subramanya Holla** said commissions range from 8% to 28%. Grievances: unapproved deep discounts, advertising charges taken without consent, opaque payout deductions, and cancellation losses — [The Week, 30 Jul 2026](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2026/07/30/wont-take-orders-anymore-why-bengaluru-restaurants-are-threatening-to-boycott-zomato-swiggy.html). More than 1,000 hotel and restaurant owners reportedly backed the move — [Business Today, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/bengaluru-restaurants-to-boycott-swiggy-zomato-from-august-15-heres-why-545884-2026-07-29)
- Swiggy agreed to refund promotional charges levied "without prior consent" after talks with BBHA, NRAI and FHRAI representatives, and the delisting deadline moved to 1 Sep. Zomato also got an extension — [Inc42, 7 Aug 2026](https://inc42.com/buzz/after-boycott-calls-swiggy-to-refund-alleged-unauthorised-charges-to-restaurants/)
- An earlier article (date not shown) quotes association president P.C. Rao and says Bengaluru has "over 24,000 small and large hotels" (a city count, not membership) — [The News Minute](https://thenewsminute.com/amp/story/karnataka/hotels-restaurants-bengaluru-soon-start-operating-24x7-179532). A lead-generation site's claim that BBHA represents "5000+ restaurants" is unverified — [D7 Lead Finder](https://d7leadfinder.com/app/prospector/23223220/713d5a86bbf1abfa6fe0296b9d05d325/)

**NRAI and FHRAI**

- NRAI launched a Mangaluru & Western Karnataka chapter on 4 Jun 2026 (chapter head Prashanth Rao Aroor) — [Roastbrief](https://roastbrief.us/nrai-expands-presence-in-karnataka-with-launch-of-mangaluru-western-karnataka-chapter/)

**Expos and events in Bengaluru**
- WOHXpo (World of Hospitality, formerly Food Hospitality World) Bengaluru 2026 was held 4–6 June at Tripuravasini, Palace Grounds, with a SICA "Incredible Chef Challenge" — [UNI India](https://test.uniindia.com/news/south/wohexpo-chef-challenge/3866229.html); [Global Trade Fairs](https://globaltradefairs.com/tradefairs/event-details/world-of-hospitality-expo-2026-bengaluru-1). - A separate Food Expo 2026 was listed at BIEC, 11–13 June 2026 — [Neventum](https://www.neventum.com/tradeshows/food-expo)

**Food courts, malls, tech parks**
- (2020) DotPe served 12 F&B brands in the Phoenix Marketcity Bengaluru food court under one mall-level deal — [Restaurant India](https://www.restaurantindia.in/news/phoenix-marketcity-bengaluru-adopts-contactless-dining-and-take-away-team-up-with-dotpe.n19401)
- HungerBox (Bengaluru) already digitises corporate cafeterias (ordering, payment, desk delivery) — [YourStory, 2020](https://yourstory.com/2020/03/paytm-growthstory-kris-gopalakrishnan-backed-bengaluru-b2b-food-startup-hungerbox); [Restaurant India, 2020](https://www.restaurantindia.in/news/food-tech-firm-hungerbox-launches-desk-ordering-feature-due-to-coronavirus-scare.n19185)

**Influencers**
- Bengaluru food creators on Instagram include Shiva Rai (~350.7k followers) and a Kannada food channel (~312.1k) — [Modash](https://www.modash.io/find-influencers/india/bangalore/food)
- See §6 for rates.

**Market size**
- Older Zomato dataset analyses put Bengaluru at roughly 12,000 listed restaurants (dataset ~2019; not a current count) — [Towards Data Science](https://towardsdatascience.com/zomato-bangalore-data-analysis-6ee83652890f/)

### Inferences
- The BBHA–aggregator dispute (Jul–Sep 2026) is the most useful Bengaluru hook. Owners are publicly angry about paying 8–28% on delivery. DineView's pitch can position dine-in tech as the place where the restaurant keeps 100% of the bill and grows it (upsell, desserts, add-ons). BBHA leadership (S Subramanya Holla) is the obvious first association meeting. An association-endorsed "member pilot" offer is a low-cost route to a first cohort of 20–50 outlets.
- Mall food courts and tech-park food courts are cluster deals: one landlord or operator decision covers 10+ counters. DotPe's 2020 Phoenix deal is the precedent. HungerBox already holds corporate cafeterias, so tech parks (Manyata, Embassy, Bagmane) may be contested or need a partnership. AR "see your dish" is less compelling for counter-service food courts than for sit-down dining, so these suit QR ordering more than the 3D feature.
- The event calendar points to a launch in Q1 2027 and a presence at WOHXpo Bengaluru in about June 2027 (if the June pattern holds). By then the company should have case studies to show.

### Gaps
- The current NRAI Bengaluru chapter head and its event calendar were not found.
- No named Bengaluru hospitality consultants or their referral fee norms were found.
- Nothing confirmed vendors or operators at Manyata, Embassy Tech Village or Bagmane food courts.
- No Bengaluru food-festival calendar (e.g. Bengaluru food festivals in 2026) was found.
- No list of hotel chains open to tech pilots was found.

## 4. Pilot design: free-pilot length, metrics restaurants care about, proving AOV/upsell lift

### Takeaway
The only quasi-experimental AR-menu evidence is the Oxford/QReal study (circa 2019). AR users ordered dessert 41.2% of the time vs 18% for the control group, and spent 112% more on dessert. Vendor claims of 10–20% average-check lift from digital or QR menus are common but unsourced. Sample-size arithmetic (below) shows that a single-outlet 30-day pilot cannot statistically prove a ~5% whole-bill AOV lift. It can prove a large lift in attach rate for specific items (dessert, add-ons, featured dishes). Design pilots around attach rates, with table-level A/B testing.

### Cited Findings
- Oxford University study with QReal (the article says "last year", reported Aug 2020): diners using AR menus that showed virtual food on the table were "significantly more likely to order dessert", 41.2% vs 18% for the control. Dessert spend was $2.93 vs $1.38 (+112%). The effect held across ages, sexes and prior AR familiarity. Sample size and methodology were not published, QReal was a partner, and the article says it "doesn't prove outright" that AR raises sales — [The Spoon, Aug 2020](https://thespoon.tech/as-menus-move-to-mobile-phones-research-shows-ar-could-drive-more-sales/)
- QReal (formerly Kabaq) case studies "suggest" a 10–20% AOV lift; "verified Indian data" was limited as of June 2026. Worked example (illustrative): Rs 1,800 average bill × 90 covers × a 5% bump = Rs 8,100/day, which would recover a Rs 88,560 first-year AR cost in about 11 days — [Petpooja blog, Jun 2026](https://blog.petpooja.com/glossary/ar-menu/)
- Indian restaurants saw a "10–20% higher average check value" after adopting digital menus or QR codes (attributed to Comit Solution, no year or method) — [Restroworks blog](https://www.restroworks.com/blog/indian-restaurant-industry-statistics/). A QR-menu vendor gives 18% and 22% in two different posts — [DineOpen](https://www.dineopen.com/blog/qr-code-menus-future-trend-2026)
- A Washington State University study reported that AR menus "may boost restaurant visits" (spotlight excerpt only) — [WSU Everett](https://everett.wsu.edu/?p=26111)
- SMB SaaS churn concentrates in the first 90 days (global) — [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-churn-rate-by-industry-saas/)

### Inferences
- **Sample size for whole-bill AOV (estimate):** to detect a 5% AOV lift at 95% confidence and 80% power, n per arm ≈ 2 × (1.96+0.84)² × (CV/0.05)². Assumption: bill CV (std dev ÷ mean) of about 0.6 (not sourced; measure it from the pilot outlet's POS). That gives n ≈ 2 × 7.84 × 144 ≈ **2,260 bills per arm**. At ~30 bills a night per arm, that is about 75 nights. Whole-bill AOV is too noisy for a 30-day single-outlet pilot unless the lift is 10%+ (n ≈ 565 per arm, about 19 nights at 30 bills/arm/night).
- **Sample size for attach rate (estimate):** if the dessert attach rate moves from 18% to 30% (a smaller effect than Oxford's), n per arm ≈ 7.84 × (0.18×0.82 + 0.30×0.70) ÷ 0.12² ≈ **195 bills per arm**. That is achievable in 1–2 weeks at a busy outlet. Using Oxford's effect (18%→41%), about 58 bills per arm.
- **Suggested pilot design (inference from the above):** a 30–45 day free pilot. Weeks 1–2: baseline from the existing POS, plus DineView QR ordering without AR. Weeks 3–6: alternate tables or alternate days with the AR and nutrition features on vs off. The primary metric is attach rate for 3–5 hero items (desserts, beverages, premium mains that have 3D models). Secondary metrics: AOV, table turn time (ordering speed) and waiter calls handled. Report weekly in a simple owner dashboard. The 30–45 day length matches the "first 90 days" churn risk, so convert to paid inside the window.
- Metrics owners talk about, from the BBHA grievance list and the Petpooja worked example: bill size, covers, margin kept vs aggregators, and staff load. Frame pilot results in rupees per day (e.g. "+₹X/day from desserts"), the way the Petpooja example does.

### Gaps
- No Indian controlled study of AR or 3D menus or QR ordering on AOV. All Indian lift figures are vendor-reported without methodology.
- No source on typical free-pilot length or pilot-to-paid conversion for Indian restaurant SaaS.
- No data on bill-size variance (CV) in Indian casual dining. The 0.6 is an assumption to replace with real POS data.

## 5. 3D dish capture as an operation: cost, time, tools, file sizes, AI image-to-3D

### Takeaway
Indian vendors quoted ₹1,500–4,000 per dish model in early 2026. Phone or turntable photogrammetry needs about 150–200 photos per dish, and processing time (not shooting) is the bottleneck unless done on a Mac or in the cloud. Mobile web AR needs a mesh: GLB for Android and a USDZ mesh for iOS Quick Look. Gaussian splats are not supported in iOS Quick Look, and WebXR is not available on iOS. Keep models to ~100k polygons or fewer, textures to 2048² or smaller, and a few MB per file. AI image-to-3D tools (Tripo, Meshy, Rodin, Hunyuan3D) cost cents per model, but no one has tested them on food.

### Cited Findings
**Market price per dish (India)**
- "Rs 1,500 to Rs 4,000" per 3D model, labelled "Indian vendor quotes as of early 2026". 40-item menu: Rs 60,000–1,60,000. QR standees and stickers Rs 80–150 per table. AR platform subscription Rs 40,000–60,000/yr. Typical first-year cost for the top 10–15 items Rs 70,000–1,20,000. The page gives no production time or file sizes — [Petpooja blog, Jun 2026](https://blog.petpooja.com/glossary/ar-menu/)

**Capture method and photo counts**
- Turntable tests found ~150–200 photos gave "excellent results for most objects", while more than 300 added processing time with no quality gain. Tested on one object only — [OpenScan](https://openscan.eu/blogs/news/optimizing-3d-scans-how-many-photos-do-you-really-need)
- University guidance: 36 images per rotation, about 30° elevation change between passes, at least 3–4 rotations — [Oxford Brookes TEL guide](https://telsupport.brookes.ac.uk/articles/how-do-i-create-a-3d-model-using-photogrammetry/)
- KabaQ (a food AR company) used a 43-photo set on a stable turntable and a 211-photo set on a continuous turntable, and also tested pulling frames from video — [Sketchfab blog](https://sketchfab.com/blogs/community/using-real-life-objects-virtual-augmented-reality-kabaq)
- Food tips: "Food usually is easy to scan". Shiny surfaces can be mattified with cornstarch and rubbing alcohol (this makes the food inedible). A phone photogrammetry app is fine for basic food scans. One user reported 240 photos taking about 10 hours to process on a powerful PC — [Revopoint forum](https://forum.revopoint3d.com/t/best-approach-to-food-scanning-tips-for-feature-turntable-scans/21885). A white base gives reflected fill light, and glossy foods may need cross-polarisation — [Pix-Pro food photogrammetry guide](https://www.pix-pro.com/blog/food-photogrammetry)

**Tools**
- Apple Object Capture: the WWDC23 demo shows on-device iPhone reconstruction to USDZ "in a few minutes" — [Apple WWDC23](https://developer.apple.com/wwdc23/10191/). A developer reports that on-device output is only "Preview" quality, so their team reconstructs at Medium on a Mac (anecdotal) — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/734811)
- Polycam: Pro is about $17/month billed annually or $27/month billed monthly (third-party listings vary: $17.99–27). Business is $400/seat/yr. The free plan allows unlimited LiDAR captures with watermarked output — [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/polycam/pricing). Polycam's own 2023 FAQ ($99.99/yr) now returns 404.
- KIRI Engine: free plan includes Photo Scan; monthly and annual subscriptions exist — [KIRI FAQ](https://kiriengine.app/faq/what-are-the-subscription-options). The cheapest paid plan is reportedly $17.99/month (aggregator, unverified) — [AIPicks](https://aipicks.jp/tool/kiri-engine/pricing)
- Luma AI: radiance-field capture uploads were disabled in Nov 2024 — [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/radiance-field-uploads-suspended-on-luma-ai). Whether it will continue developing 3D capture is unclear — [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/platforms/luma-ai). The App Store listing notes that Genie was sunset on 1 Jan 2026 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/DE/app/id1615849914)

**Gaussian splatting for web AR**
- An Apple DTS engineer said AR Quick Look "may not support this technique [splats] directly". Another developer saw an empty AR preview for a splat USDZ — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/804604). Needle reports that "Apple does not allow WebXR usage on iOS". Its exporter falls back to a mesh for iOS AR — [Needle forum](https://forum.needle.tools/t/gaussian-splat-ar-on-iphones/2515)

**File-size and polygon targets**
- Apple recommends about 100k polygons for best performance. Models above roughly 500k faces may fail to load. Textures should be no larger than 2048×2048 — [Sketchfab Help](https://help.sketchfab.com/en/articles/16152669-gltf-glb-and-usdz)
- A USDZ with over 40 MB of 4K PNG textures caused problems; switching to 2K JPEGs is the usual fix — [Shapr3D forum](https://discourse.shapr3d.com/t/ar-view/25137). One vendor targets under 5 MB for a GLB + USDZ pair (commercial claim) — [UFO3D](https://ufo3d.com/?p=9935)

**AI image-to-3D (2026)**
- Rankings: Tripo is ranked first overall, ahead of Meshy, Hyper3D Rodin, Hunyuan3D and TRELLIS.2 (one author's tests) — [Medium](https://medium.com/ideas-with-wings/best-image-to-3d-tools-7eea7b05eb11). Meshy is the most complete standalone tool — [Krea](https://www.krea.ai/blog/best-ai-3d-model-generators-2026). Rodin is the photorealism leader for hero assets. Tripo is the fastest (under 10 seconds). Hunyuan3D is open source and needs a GPU — [RunDiffusion](https://www.rundiffusion.com/ai-3d-model-generators)
- Image-to-3D reconstructs "a single clean silhouette far better than a scene", so a single object on a plain background works best — [Krea](https://www.krea.ai/blog/best-ai-3d-model-generators-2026)
- Cost: Meshy Pro is ~$20/month for 1,000 credits (~50 textured models, ~$0.40/model). Tripo is ~$0.21/model (comparison by a competitor; plan details conflict across sources) — [Sloyd](https://sloyd.ai/blog/3d-ai-price-comparison)
- None of the sources tested food (glossy sauces, translucent drinks, garnishes, steam).

**Freelance 3D cleanup (India)**
- Indian Blender freelancers list about $12–20/hour: Mumbai artist $20/hr — [Hubstaff Talent](https://hubstafftalent.net/profiles/asad-sayyed); Freelancer.in $20/hr — [Freelancer.in](https://www.freelancer.in/u/onkargraphics); Fiverr $12/hr basic modelling — [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/radanimate). Open-project bids average about $5/hr — [Freelancer.com](https://www.freelancer.com/projects/blender-3d/private-collaboration-for-julianto). Modelled salary-equivalent for a mid-level Blender contractor is ~₹9 L/yr (₹5.5–15.1 L), with platform fees of 10–20% on top — [Flexiple](https://flexiple.com/salary/blender)

### Inferences
- **Per-dish capture time (estimate, inputs shown):** about 150–200 photos (OpenScan). Assumption: a phone on a tripod with a manual or motorised turntable takes about 1 photo every 2–3 seconds, so 5–10 minutes of shooting. Add about 10 minutes for plating a fresh portion, lighting and a quick check (assumption). That is ~15–20 minutes per dish on site. Processing (minutes on an Apple Silicon Mac via Object Capture, up to hours on a PC per the forum) runs off-site or overnight. **Dishes per day:** kitchens can usually only plate for capture outside service (assumption: a 3–4 hour afternoon window), so ~10–15 dishes per restaurant visit and about 1 restaurant per day per capture person. Validate both numbers in the first 3 shoots.
- **Per-dish cost to DineView (estimate):** capture labour (an in-house person at about ₹25–35k/month, by analogy with the field-sales pay band; assumption) ÷ ~200–300 dishes/month ≈ ₹100–175/dish. Cleanup at 0.5–1.5 h × $12–20/h = $6–30 (≈₹500–2,550 at an assumed ₹85/USD; check the current rate). App and AI tool costs are under ₹50/dish. Total ≈ **₹600–2,800 per dish**, against market quotes of ₹1,500–4,000. A one-time capture fee in the ₹1,500–2,500 per dish range (or bundling the top 10–15 dishes) looks viable with margin. Margin depends mostly on how much cleanup each dish needs.
- **Pipeline choice:** use photogrammetry (Object Capture, KIRI or Polycam) to a textured mesh, then decimate to ≤100k triangles with 2K textures, export GLB (Android Scene Viewer / model-viewer) and USDZ (iOS Quick Look), and target a few MB per file. Use Gaussian splats only for in-browser 3D previews, not AR, because Quick Look cannot show them and iOS has no WebXR. Avoid depending on Luma for capture. Test AI image-to-3D (Tripo, Meshy, Hunyuan3D, Rodin) as a fallback for simple, solid dishes (burgers, cakes, breads) where 1–4 photos are enough. Run a blind side-by-side against photogrammetry before relying on it.
- **Real-size requirement:** photogrammetry output needs scale calibration (a known-size plate or marker in frame) so that "real-size on the table" is correct. AI image-to-3D output has no inherent scale and must be scaled by hand. (This follows from the methods above; no source tested it.)

### Gaps
- No measured time-per-dish or dishes-per-day figure from a real food-AR operation (Kabaq/QReal did not publish throughput).
- No food-specific quality test of AI image-to-3D tools.
- No official model-viewer, Apple or Google file-size limit; the targets above are community and vendor rules of thumb.
- Official current Polycam and KIRI pricing pages were not retrieved (third-party figures only).

## 7. Payment collection norms for Indian SMB SaaS (monthly vs annual, UPI AutoPay, GST)

### Takeaway
Indian restaurant POS vendors typically bill per outlet per year, often with a discounted renewal (Petpooja ₹10,000 then ₹7,500) or a "pay 10 months, use 12" annual deal (GoFrugal). Monthly billing exists. UPI AutoPay mandates can auto-debit up to ₹15,000 per charge without a PIN (24-hour pre-debit notice), which covers most per-outlet monthly fees. SaaS attracts 18% GST. GST registration is required above ₹20 lakh turnover for services in Karnataka, and e-invoicing applies above ₹5 crore turnover.

### Cited Findings
**Billing cadence**
- Petpooja: Rs 10,000 for the first year per outlet, then Rs 7,500/yr renewal (listing) — [Techjockey](https://techjockey.com/detail/petpooja-pos); the official page prices per year per outlet (USD) — [Petpooja pricing](https://petpooja.com/pricing)
- GoFrugal offers monthly or annual billing ("Pay for 10 months and use for an year!") and a one-time licence with yearly renewal — [GoFrugal pricing](https://www.gofrugal.com/pricing.html)
- ZaykaBill (Indian restaurant POS) offers annual and monthly billing with a 30-day trial — [GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/software/zaykabill). FoodHisaab prices yearly and has a free tier — [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/foodhisaab/)
- AR-menu platform subscriptions in India are quoted annually at Rs 40,000–60,000 — [Petpooja blog, Jun 2026](https://blog.petpooja.com/glossary/ar-menu/)

**UPI AutoPay (e-mandates)**
- Customers authorise the mandate with a UPI PIN at setup. At least 24 hours before each recurring debit, the customer must get an SMS or app notification with the exact amount and an option to cancel. The default and maximum auto-chargeable mandate amount is ₹15,000. Amount type can be "maximum" (variable up to the cap) or "fixed". Mandates can last up to 40 years (scheme maximum) — [Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/upi/upi-autopay)
- Recurring transactions above ₹15,000 need PIN entry each time. The issuing bank sends a UPI Collect request — [Juspay docs](https://juspay.io/mandates/docs/how-to-integrate/enable-mandate-recurring-debits)
- NPCI's Dec 2023 circular raised the no-PIN limit to ₹1,00,000 only for specific categories (mutual funds, insurance, credit-card bill payments), not SaaS — [PhonePe Business](https://business.phonepe.com/articles/how-to-accept-subscription-payments-in-india-upi-auto-pay-vs-e-nach-vs-cards)
- Paddle caps any checkout containing a recurring item at ₹15,000 for UPI AutoPay (platform rule, 2026) — [Paddle changelog](https://developer.paddle.com/changelog/2026/upi-autopay/)

**GST and invoicing**
- SaaS falls in the 18% GST slab (SAC 9983), with no exemption — [PayPro Global](https://payproglobal.com/saas-sales-tax/india/)
- Registration is required above ₹20 lakh annual turnover for services (₹10 lakh in special-category states; Karnataka is not one). One guide claims inter-state SaaS supply needs registration from the first invoice. This conflicts with the general threshold rule and should be checked with a CA — [Kintsugi](https://trykintsugi.com/sales-tax-guides/apac/india)
- E-invoicing is mandatory above ₹5 crore aggregate turnover since 1 Aug 2023 (CBIC Notification 10/2023–Central Tax). No 2026 change was found — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/threshold-limit-for-generating-e-invoices-is-reduced-to-5-crores-notification/)

**Guest-side payments context**
- A 2026 bill would remove UPI's statutory zero-MDR guarantee. Reported P2M rate is 0.4% capped at ₹300, with merchant payments up to ₹2,000 staying free (newsletter analysis; a proposal, not enacted fact) — [All About Fintech](https://allaboutfintech.beehiiv.com/p/upi-mdr-2026-what-bill-no-150-actually-changes); background — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/upis-monetisation-moment-why-mdr-is-back-on-the-table/)
- PhonePe launched its own POS device, following Pine Labs, BharatPe and Paytm into hardware — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=405295)

### Inferences
- A per-outlet monthly fee up to ₹15,000 (well above likely DineView pricing) can run on UPI AutoPay with no PIN after the first authorisation. That makes monthly billing workable for restaurants that won't prepay a year. Offer annual prepay at a discount (the "10 for 12" norm, about 17% off) to cut churn exposure and improve cash flow. Collect the one-time 3D capture fee upfront or 50/50 (capture / go-live).
- Price display: Indian SMBs compare pre-GST numbers (DineOpen's Petpooja estimate is quoted "before GST"). State prices "+18% GST" explicitly. Restaurants registered under GST can usually claim input tax credit on the SaaS fee. Restaurants on the composition scheme cannot. That last point is inference from general GST rules and was not verified in a source here; confirm with a CA.
- Below ₹20 lakh turnover DineView may not need GST registration. However, GST-registered restaurant customers will want a GST invoice for input credit, so registering early is probably commercially necessary. This is an inference; confirm with a CA.

### Gaps
- No primary NPCI circular was retrieved for the ₹15,000 UPI AutoPay limit or for retry rules. Figures come from Stripe, Juspay and PhonePe documentation.
- No data on what share of Indian restaurants actually prepay annually vs monthly, or on UPI AutoPay mandate failure and churn rates for SMB SaaS.
- Inter-state registration and input-credit points need professional (CA) confirmation.
